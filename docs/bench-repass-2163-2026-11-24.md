# FreeGem re-pass record — 10,000 sites on 2.16.3 (2026-11-24)

Full re-pass of the first-pass corpus (docs/bench-live-10k-and-feature-tests.md) on build 2.16.3 — brotli decode shim, plus the 2.16.2 per-seed wall-clock budget (--seed-budget, #103).

**Outcome: 6,082 / 10,000 readable (60.8%) vs 3,734 (37.3%) pass 1 — +23.5 points, beating the ~55-57% projection.**

## Method

- Carried over (3,734): pass-1 readable seeds (extraction path byte-identical in 2.16.3; spot re-check 315 seeds -> 310/315 = 98.4% stable).
- Re-attempted: every non-readable pass-1 seed through the full ladder, --max-attempts 8, --seed-budget 120, 48 dials (3 shards x 16 workers), 10 s socket timeout, keep-alive on, robots.txt never fetched or consulted (v3 policy), anonymous default UA, AI/distill/stealth rungs off.
- Wall treatment: all 1,532 pass-1 403s re-attempted; 143 (9.3%) passed honestly — profile variants + archive-fallback rung (e.g. redbull.com, resume.com: 403-with-wayback in pass 1, now extracting). 1,389 stayed recorded as refusals. No CAPTCHA solving, no identity rotation, no wall cracking.

Reconciliation (recorded as submitted): rows sum re-attempted 6,216 vs headline 6,266 (delta 50 in the pass-1 other split); new-readable rows sum to 6,084 vs headline 6,082 (delta 2). Neither affects the attribution.

## Operational findings

1. A plain campaign run re-run (no --resume/--retry-failed) re-attempts the WHOLE queue regardless of existing results — recovery chunking needs a fresh state DB per invocation or checkpoint manifests. Filed upstream as a follow-up issue.
2. Sandbox egress ceiling: >=96 concurrent dials throttle to effective stalls (0 rows/590 s); 48 dials (3x16) was the sweet spot; 16 fine.
3. --seed-budget 120 earned its keep on this wall/dead-heavy corpus.
